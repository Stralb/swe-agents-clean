\suppbox{Attribution protocol A: reconstruct and map findings (original 122 instances)}{%
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.13}%
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{0.8cm}Y@{}}
\textbf{01} & \textbf{Assemble / deduplicate.} Collect issue, PR, diff and tool evidence; merge duplicate records of the same finding within an instance, not distinct rules or advisories (832 raw references $\to$ 605 findings).\\
\textbf{02} & \textbf{Propose responsible operation.} Give the language model the finding, tool evidence and PR diff, but not the issue or PR description. Record its proposed finding-producing code or dependency operation.\\
\textbf{03} & \textbf{Verify dependency transitions.} At the true merge base and PR head, check 987 candidate package/version transitions: 274 introduced, 697 already present and 16 unreproduced. These checks excluded 12 initially positive instances after four earlier merge-base exclusions, supported 142 retained dependency links, and resolved 36 previously unresolved links, all associated with case 13918. Individual link-basis fields do not distinguish those 36. Comparable coverage is not established for the 70 later positives. These checks address PR attribution, not advisory validity.\\
\textbf{04} & \textbf{Apply evidence gate.} Retain a mapping only with an identified changed file, changed line/hunk and explanation connecting the change to the finding; otherwise leave unresolved. All 605 mapped findings passed the recorded gate (Table~\ref{tab:mapping}).\\
\end{tabularx}}

\section{RQ2 Matching and Sensitivity}\label{sec:s5}

\suppbox{Matched-control procedure (RQ2)}{%
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.12}%
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{0.8cm}Y@{}}
\textbf{01} & Define scanner-compatible candidate controls: same-repository edges require coverage of the positive's completed checks; cross-repository edges require identical completed-check signatures.\\
\textbf{02} & Apply the frozen date and change-size calipers (Section~\ref{sec:rq2-design}); exclude ineligible edges.\\
\textbf{03} & Solve one-to-one matching without replacement, prioritising maximum pair count, then same-repository pairs, then minimum normalised date/size distance, in that order.\\
\textbf{04} & Freeze the pair map; only then join the issue-wording annotations. No pair-specific re-annotation. Figure~\ref{fig:rq2-method} shows the matching stages.\\
\end{tabularx}}

\suppbox{Issue-wording codes and inference boundary}{%
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.12}%
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{2.3cm}Y@{}}
\textbf{Problem only} & Problem or goal without a proposed implementation.\\
\textbf{Suggests solution} & Approach proposed, without fixing the method.\\
\textbf{Explicit instruction} & A concrete implementation instruction; distinct from H (exact mechanism prescribed).\\
\textbf{Statistical test} & Paired $3\times3$ Stuart--Maxwell test with matched pairs as units; categories are not a numeric score. Explicit-versus-other proportions are descriptive.\\
\textbf{Multiple tests} & The archive lists nine primary comparisons in a Holm family. Only the issue-wording statistic was independently recomputed from the frozen contingency table. Its archived adjusted p-value equals nine times its unadjusted p-value, consistent with the smallest of nine p-values; the other comparisons and family composition remain source-reported.\\
\textbf{Interpretation} & Issue-wording specificity is not a measurement of security requirements or the precise risky mechanism.\\
